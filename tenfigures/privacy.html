Privacy Policy — Ten Figures

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Português

This policy explains what data the game Ten Figures (Android app com.lippegames.tenfigures), by Lippe Studios, uses and how.

1. The data the game uses

  • Your game, saved on your phone: your money, your companies, your staff, your to-do list and your best record. This file stays only on your device, inside the app's own storage. We do not receive it.

2. What the game does NOT do

  • It does not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • It does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • It has no ads and no in-app purchases in this version.
  • It does not use the internet to play.

3. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game (or clearing its data in the Android settings) deletes everything it saved. There is nothing stored on our side.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

5. Changes

If a future version starts using a service that handles data (for example Google Play Games, ads or purchases), this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date at the top.
