Milim Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · Versão em português

In short: Milim asks for no sign-up and does not keep who you are. Your matches stay on your device. Playing with friends needs the internet, and then the nickname you chose, the room name and your guesses go through the servers that make the match happen. The game asks for no account and no e-mail. It shows ads between quick matches, and for that it uses the device's advertising ID — never in the daily or weekly word, nor when playing with friends. If you send Feedback from Settings, your nickname and the text you wrote are sent. Milim Premium is bought through Google Play: the payment is handled entirely by Google, and the game never sees your card details.

What stays only on your device

The game saves a file inside its own folder on your phone with:

  • the name or nickname you type;
  • your points, matches, wins, streaks and medals (one set per language);
  • your preferences for sound, music, vibration, language and color theme;
  • whether this device is Premium.

Playing the word of the day, the word of the week or a quick match, none of this leaves the device. Uninstalling the game deletes the file with it.

What is sent when you play with friends

Room battles happen on several phones at the same time, and that does not work without a server. When you join or create a room, the game sends:

  • the name or nickname you typed — it is what the other players see in the room and in the rankings;
  • an anonymous number for this device, created by the game itself, to tell one player from another inside the match;
  • the room name and password, when you create one;
  • the words you try during the battle, how many tries you used and how long you took.

That anonymous number is not your phone number, is not linked to any account of yours and cannot identify you outside the game. No e-mail, phone number, contact list, location or photo is sent.

Who stores it

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) — the only one. It runs the whole room: who is in it, how many fit, the password, and the battle's progress — who solved each word, how fast, and the team guesses in relay mode. The game uses anonymous sign-in, which asks for no account. Policy: unity.com/legal.
  • Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) — picks, for each ad, which network shows it: Unity Ads, ironSource (both from Unity) or Google AdMob (from Google). To show and count the ads, these networks use the device's advertising ID, the IP address (from which they infer the country and approximate region), technical device data (model, system, language) and how you interact with the ad (whether you watched it to the end, whether you tapped it). The advertising ID belongs to Android, and you can reset or turn it off in the phone settings, under Google › Ads. In Europe, the UK and Switzerland the game tells the networks there is no consent, and the ads are not personalized. Policies: unity.com/legal (Unity Ads and ironSource) and policies.google.com (Google AdMob).
  • Google Play (Google) — handles the Milim Premium purchase: charges, keeps the receipt and handles refunds. Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Forms (Google) — receives the feedback sent from Settings, and only that (see below). Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The nickname, the guesses and the anonymous device number are not used for advertising or audience measurement — they only serve to make the match happen. The ads receive none of them: what they use is described in the Unity LevelPlay item above.

How long it is kept

A room stops existing when the last player leaves it, and the server also deletes rooms that sit idle. The record of a battle — the names, the words and the times — stays in the database after it ends, and is deleted periodically. If you want the record of one of your battles deleted sooner, write to the contact at the end of this page.

Pick a nickname, not your full name

The name you type is what the other players in the room see. It is free: choose a nickname. The game does not check or validate it, and does not need to know who you really are.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share with friends, the game builds a text with the colored squares of your match and hands that text to the app you choose (WhatsApp, Telegram, e-mail, whatever is installed). Milim sends nothing by itself and does not know who you sent it to.

Feedback

Settings has a Feedback button. It only sends something if you write and tap Send. Then these go:

  • the nickname you wrote (any nickname, or blank);
  • the text of your suggestion;
  • the game version, so we know which version you mean.

It goes to a Google Form and lands in a spreadsheet only the developer of Milim reads, used only to improve the game. No e-mail, phone, location, photo or anonymous device number is sent. Do not write personal data in your feedback. If you want one of your suggestions deleted, write to the contact at the end of this page.

Purchases (Milim Premium)

Milim Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google is who charges, keeps the receipt and handles refunds; Milim does not receive your card number, the payer's name or the billing address. On launch, the game asks Google Play whether your account owns Premium and stores on the device only the answer (yes or no) and the purchase code, so the same purchase is not granted twice. None of this is sent anywhere else.

Game updates

The game reads a file on the official website with settings of the game itself (for example, whether ads are on). That read carries no data of yours — it is a read only. New versions arrive through Google Play.

Children

Milim is a word game suitable for all ages. It asks for no personal data and has no free chat: in rooms, the only things a player writes and others see are their nickname and the words they tried, which are words from the game's own dictionary. Feedback is not shown to other players: only the developer reads it.

Changes to this policy

If one day the game starts collecting any data, this page will be updated before that happens, and the date at the top will change.
